<task type="auto">
  <name>Implement date-filtered Sales Report with full VAT categorization</name>
  <files>src/main/services/reports.service.ts, src/preload/index.ts, src/main/index.ts</files>
  <action>
    Implement `ReportsService.getSalesReport(startDateStr: string, endDateStr: string)`:
    - Query sales journal entries (`reference_no` with `INV-` or `SYS-` or accounts in `4010`, `4020`, `4040`, `1200`) within date bounds.
    - For each invoice / transaction, extract:
      - Date
      - Invoice No. (`reference_no`)
      - Patient / Payee Name & TIN
      - Line of Business / Description
      - Gross Sales Amount
      - Vatable Sales (gross / 1.12 when line has Output VAT Account `2020`)
      - VAT-Exempt Sales (consultations / exempt services without VAT)
      - Zero-Rated Sales (if applicable)
      - Output VAT (Account `2020` credit amount)
      - Discounts (Senior / PWD Account `4030` debit)
      - Net AR / Cash Received (Account `1010` / `1200`)
    - Calculate running cumulative sales total and period summary subtotals.
    - Expose IPC channel `reports:get-sales-report` in main and preload.
  </action>
