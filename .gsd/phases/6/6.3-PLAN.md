# Plan 6.3: Accounting Calendar & Period Closing UI

## Objective
Build an intuitive, interactive "Accounting Calendar & Books Closing" interface inside `SystemSettingsView.tsx` allowing accountants and managers to view fiscal periods, inspect net income and revenue/expense metrics per period, execute 1-click period-end books closing, and safely reopen closed periods with PIN authorization.
