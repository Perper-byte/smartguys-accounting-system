# Plan 6.1: Dynamic user-configurable Accounting Calendar (Fiscal Year Support)

## Objective
Implement a mechanism that allows users to configure the starting month of their accounting calendar (Fiscal Year) so that the generated periods span correctly based on the user's fiscal year settings rather than strictly defaulting to a January-December calendar year.

## Context
Load these files for context:
- .gsd/SPEC.md
- src/main/services/accounting-period.service.ts
- src/renderer/src/components/SystemSettingsView.tsx
